# Searching for "acme" finds nothing, but "Acme" works

**What I did**

On the Pipeline page I typed `acme` in the search box to find our contact at Acme Consultores.

**What I expected**

To see Beatriz Lopes from Acme Consultores in the list. I did not think capital letters would matter, they do not in any other search box I use.

**What actually happened**

Nothing at all, every stage section was empty. When I typed `Acme` with a capital A she showed up straight away. Same thing with names: `beatriz` finds nobody, `Beatriz` finds her. Half the team types in lowercase, so right now they think leads are missing.
